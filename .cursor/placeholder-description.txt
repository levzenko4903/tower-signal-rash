Tower Signal Rush is an offline timing climb where a relay beam sweeps the shaft and you tap to lock it onto the tower, lining it up with the block below and staying clear of the orange hazard zones. The tap is skill: a centered lock is a perfect that widens the beam and builds a score chain, a partial overlap still stacks a thinner relay, and a miss or a hazard hit spends integrity until the uplink is lost, the beam overloads, or the signal times out, though you can also end the run from pause and keep the score already earned. Luck only places the orange zones, which show up from sector 2 in random spots and become more common as later sectors speed the beam up. Rewards are the run score from locks, perfects, chains, and sector bonuses, added to the Signal total on the menu, plus cosmetic beam colours in Signal Trails that unlock when your best score passes fixed marks. Cash-out does not exist: Signal stays on the device and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money, and the offline build has no shop and no in-app purchases, so Signal and trail colours cannot be bought.
